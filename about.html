About Heritage Henna

At Heritage Henna, every design tells a story — one rooted in tradition, creativity, and passion.

What began as a simple hobby when I was 15 has grown into something much more meaningful. I started creating henna designs for fun, and soon friends and family began asking me to do theirs too. The joy on their faces and the appreciation for my art inspired me to turn my passion into something official — and that’s how Heritage Henna was born.

Today, I create henna designs that blend traditional elegance with modern artistry. Whether it’s for weddings, festivals, or special celebrations, each design is drawn by hand, crafted with care, and made using natural, skin-safe henna.

For me, henna isn’t just decoration — it’s an expression of culture, emotion, and beauty. My goal is to make every client feel confident, connected, and radiant through this timeless art form.

Welcome to Heritage Henna — where every pattern carries a piece of heritage and heart.
